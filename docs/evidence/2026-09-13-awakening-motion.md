# Twelve-frame awakening and original-creation introduction

Date: 2026-09-13. Released: 0.0.8. This artwork refinement keeps the qualified
Mac Codex source-control scope and the [0.0.7 release](2026-09-13-entity-awakening.md)
intact. It does not expand native providers, platforms or remote-plugin control.

## Behavior and saved-version boundaries

- The plugin fixes twelve frames, their row/column order and one common scale.
  Nine intermediate drawings unfold arms, knees and legs between UNSEAL and
  TRUEFORM, in reverse when closing. The mechanical rig retains its 49 cels.
- The new immutable `entity-awakening/v2` profile contains three canonical
  endpoints and nine `unfold` references. Old three-pose profiles, single-image
  works and v1/v2 creation places retain their contracts. Retried old creation
  UUIDs reopen their original places; new places issue the twelve-frame guide.
- White/silver and amber prepared versions are kept in new `silver-v2` and
  `amber-v2` directories. Original source PNGs and the built-in image-generation
  prompts remain with their normalized assets. The prior directories were not
  overwritten; the retained normalizer reproduces their PNG bytes exactly.
- TRUEFORM crests spread outward through expanding silhouettes, light streaks
  and bloom. Faces and solid bodies remain separate from the light; effects-off
  and reduced-motion settings retain canonical static endpoints.
- The introduction leads with “メカも、擬人化も。好きな姿を、いつものAIに。”
  It pairs the default with a humanoid and offers adjacent 白銀/琥珀 buttons and
  通常/限定解除/零式 mode buttons. Connected public previews expose effects too.
- A scene is marked ready only for its accepted artwork ID and image loader.
  A newly selected label cannot briefly present the previous character as ready.
